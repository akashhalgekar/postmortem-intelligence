# Label printer outage at outbound dock

## Summary
The thermal label printer at the outbound dock failed during the evening shift. 140 orders missed the carrier cutoff.

## Root Cause
The only label printer on the dock had no spare, and the print server queue had no failover to another printer.

## Contributing Factors
Preventive maintenance on the printer was overdue by two months.

## Actions Taken
Moved label printing to the returns desk printer, bought a spare, added a failover queue.

## Impact
140 orders shipped one day late.

## Lessons Learned
Single points of failure on the dock need a spare on site and a tested failover.

## KPIs
On time ship; orders missing carrier cutoff
